01 THE PRACTICE

Architecture that begins with what is already there.

NORTH/FORM is an independent architecture and interiors practice based in London.

We design residential, cultural and commercial spaces with an emphasis on proportion, natural light and honest materials.

Our work avoids unnecessary gestures. Every decision should improve the way a place is understood, used and experienced.
